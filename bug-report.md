# Bug Report – Krunker.io

## Bug ID
BUG-001

## Title
Low visual contrast between weapon model and ground texture

## Severity
Medium

## Environment
Game: Krunker.io  
Platform: Browser  
OS: Windows  
Browser: Chrome  

## Description
During gameplay the weapon model visually blends with the ground texture.

Low contrast between textures makes the weapon difficult to distinguish from the environment.

## Steps to reproduce
1 Open https://krunker.io  
2 Join a match  
3 Move around the map  
4 Observe weapon model against ground texture  

## Expected result
Weapon model should remain clearly visible against the ground surface.

## Actual result
Weapon visually blends with the ground texture.
